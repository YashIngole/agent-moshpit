# Agent Moshpit v0.5.5

Voice input, made fast and live.

- **Ten times faster recognition.** The speech engine in every earlier build ran without the processor's vector instructions: 11 seconds of speech took 49 seconds to recognise with Small. It now uses AVX2 on Intel and AMD, and recognises a phrase in about 0.15 s with Base and 0.6 s with Small on a six-core laptop. On a processor without AVX2 (Intel before 2013, AMD before 2015), voice says so instead of starting.
- **Words appear as you speak.** A card over the terminals shows the phrase you are saying as it is recognised, about twice a second.
- **Typed as you pause.** Each phrase goes into the terminal when you pause after it, so stopping leaves only the last few words to type. Voice still never presses Enter.
- **Hold to talk.** Hold **Ctrl+Shift+Space** while you speak and let go to finish, or tap it to talk hands-free and tap again to finish.
- **No more stuck Voice button.** Recognition used to run for up to a minute after stopping, and cancelling could not interrupt it, which left the Voice button greyed out. Recognition now only works on the audio that was said, rather than a padded 30-second window, and finishes in well under a second.
- **The terminals stay still.** The voice card floats over the work instead of pushing the terminals down, so they are no longer resized each time it appears.
- **Easier setup.** If the chosen model is not downloaded but the other one is, setup offers to use it. Recordings can now run for five minutes.

Recognition and timing were checked with recorded speech (whisper.cpp's sample) and synthetic noise, and in the desktop app with that recording in place of a microphone. Voice has not been tried with a live microphone in a noisy room. Update from **More** or use the installers below. The existing [code-signing limitations](https://github.com/YashIngole/agent-moshpit#install) remain.

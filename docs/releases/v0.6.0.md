# Agent Moshpit v0.6.0

A calmer office that says each thing once, and voice input that types as you speak.

## The office says less, and knows more

- **A desk says each thing once.** The line under a name no longer repeats the status ("working", then "Working"). It appears only when there is something new: what the agent reported it is doing, what its program asked, or what went wrong. How long they have been at it now comes before the program's name, which shows only when you run more than one program.
- **More of the floor fits.** The empty "add an agent" desk in every room is now a **+** on the room's sign, and the room head counts are gone. Seven agents in three rooms now fit on a 1280×800 screen, the one in trouble included.
- **Your terminals stay still when a hand goes up.** While terminals are open, whoever needs you is an amber chip in the top bar instead of a band that pushed the terminals down. On the floor the band is as before, without its label line, and it shows what the program asked when it says.
- **Go to whoever needs you from anywhere.** `Ctrl+Shift+A`, or the chip, opens the agent who has waited longest, even from inside a terminal; press it again for the next. A name under **also waiting** now opens that terminal in one click.
- **A click never hides someone waiting.** A plain click on a desk no longer puts away a terminal whose agent needs you or is in trouble; the calmest other one makes room. On a screen wide enough for both, it opens beside the others.
- **The terminals get the room.** With two or more terminals, more than ten agents, or a laptop-sized window, the floor beside them starts as the strip of names, until you choose a width.
- **The top bar counts only what wants a look:** how many are done and how many are in trouble. Who is at work is on the floor. "back to the floor" is now **hide terminals**, the key hints moved into tooltips, and the microphone is an icon.
- **New agent asks who, what and where.** The folder is filled in with the one you most likely mean: the terminal you are in, else where you last started an agent. Name, model, effort and permissions fold into one **More options** line that says what they are set to.
- **Shorter menus.** One line per item with its key at the end, and a hint only where something ends or is kept. Settings in **More** are tick boxes. A terminal's right-click menu ends in **More for (name)…** instead of repeating the whole desk menu.
- **Agents' own words reach you.** What an agent reports through the office's MCP tools shows in its pane's header, and a done notification carries its last report instead of "Open their terminal to see what they did."
- **The "something new" dot means something.** It no longer sits on every agent at work, who prints all the time.

## Voice input, made fast and live

- **Ten times faster recognition.** The speech engine in every earlier build ran without the processor's vector instructions: 11 seconds of speech took 49 seconds to recognise with Small. It now uses AVX2 on Intel and AMD, and recognises a phrase in about 0.15 s with Base and 0.6 s with Small on a six-core laptop. On a processor without AVX2 (Intel before 2013, AMD before 2015), voice says so instead of starting.
- **Words appear as you speak.** A card over the terminals shows the phrase you are saying as it is recognised, about twice a second.
- **Typed as you pause.** Each phrase goes into the terminal when you pause after it, so stopping leaves only the last few words to type. Voice still never presses Enter.
- **Hold to talk.** Hold **Ctrl+Shift+Space** while you speak and let go to finish, or tap it to talk hands-free and tap again to finish.
- **No more stuck Voice button.** Recognition used to run for up to a minute after stopping, and cancelling could not interrupt it. Recognition now only works on the audio that was said, rather than a padded 30-second window, and finishes in well under a second.
- **The terminals stay still.** The voice card floats over the work instead of pushing the terminals down.
- **Easier setup.** If the chosen model is not downloaded but the other one is, setup offers to use it. Recordings can now run for five minutes.

The office changes were checked in the demo office in a browser at sizes from 340 to 2400 pixels wide. Recognition and timing were checked with recorded speech and synthetic noise, not with a live microphone in a noisy room. Update from **More** or use the installers below. The existing [code-signing limitations](https://github.com/YashIngole/agent-moshpit#install) remain.

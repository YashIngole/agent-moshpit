# Agent Moshpit v0.7.0

Voice input that keeps up with you: a streaming speech model on your computer, or the cloud provider of your choice with your own key.

## A new speech engine on your computer

- **Words are typed as you say them.** Voice now runs NVIDIA's Nemotron Speech Streaming model, which is made for live dictation: it types each word within about a second of your saying it, and never rewrites it. There are no more bursts at each pause, and no shown words changing under you. When you stop, the last words follow in a fraction of a second.
- **Much better at code talk.** On our test commands ("run cargo clippy with all targets", "ask Codex to review the pull request"), it made one mistake in 81 words.
- **A crash can't take your terminals with it.** The speech engine runs in a helper process of its own. If it ever fails, that recording says so, the words already typed stay, and the next recording starts a new one.
- **Choose the size.** The recommended model is a 730 MB download and uses about 1.1 GB of memory while loaded; a smaller 475 MB one uses about 0.85 GB. Both come from Hugging Face when you ask, are checked before use, and an interrupted download resumes where it stopped.

## Or the cloud, with your own key

- **Deepgram, OpenAI, AssemblyAI or ElevenLabs.** Choose **Cloud · your own key** in Voice input, pick a provider, and paste its API key. You pay the provider directly; Agent Moshpit takes no cut and has no account of its own there.
- **Your key stays in your system's keychain.** It is checked with the provider first, then kept in Windows Credential Manager, the macOS keychain or the Linux Secret Service. The window only ever sees its last four characters, and it is never written to a file.
- **You decide where your audio goes.** The panel says, before you use it, that your audio goes to that provider while you dictate, and only then. Local stays the default, and voice never switches to the cloud by itself.
- **Word hints.** Add names and terms for the provider to expect. Program names, and your desk's name, folder and branch (unless you turn that off), go along to help it spell them.

## Changed

- **Voice is English only.** The language setting is gone with the old Whisper models, which are deleted from your data folder on first start. Your other voice settings carry over.
- **On an older processor** (Intel before 2013, AMD before 2015), the local engine can't run; the panel now points you to the cloud instead of turning voice off.
- **Settings are read one at a time,** so a setting the app doesn't recognise no longer resets all the others.

Voice was checked with recorded and synthesised speech fed at speaking pace, a fan-noise clip, and a pretend cloud provider that speaks each provider's documented protocol. It was not checked with a live microphone in a noisy room, or with a real key for each cloud provider; if one misbehaves, please [open an issue](https://github.com/YashIngole/agent-moshpit/issues).

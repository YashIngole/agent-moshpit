# Agent Moshpit v0.9.0

Dictation that carries on while you look around, a microphone you can choose from the bar, and a click on a desk that only ever adds or removes that desk's terminal.

## Voice

- **A recording stays with its terminal.** Until now a recording was cancelled whenever its terminal was covered or put away: by a desk's menu, a panel, a click on another desk, or a trip back to the floor. Now it carries on until you stop or cancel it, its program ends, or the window is minimised or closed. A recording can still only be started into a terminal that is in front of you.
- **Choose the microphone from the bar.** The small arrow beside the microphone lists your inputs, with the one in use ticked. With more than one engine ready (the local model and a provider's key, say) the same menu switches between them. Choosing another while you dictate carries the recording on with it.
- **It says less.** A recording is now a small bar in the corner of the terminal its words go into: a meter, the time, the words still being decided, **Stop** and cancel. It names the agent only when their terminal is not in front, and a click on the name brings it back. Once the words are typed, nothing is left over the terminal.
- **A silent microphone is named.** If nothing is heard, the bar says which microphone hears nothing and offers **Change microphone** there.
- **The microphone no longer takes the keyboard from the terminal,** and the top bar no longer shifts when a recording starts.
- **Voice input** in the menu was cut down to what you need to set it up. What is kept, what ends a recording and how much memory the model uses are under **Privacy and limits** at its foot.

If you put a terminal away while dictating into it, the words keep going to its program, and the bar says whose they are.

## Opening terminals

- **A click adds or removes, and never replaces.** Click a desk and their terminal opens beside the others; click it again and it is put away, with its program still running. A click no longer takes the place of a terminal you had open.
- **An agent who needs you is never put away by a click.** The click takes you to their terminal, where the answer goes.
- **Swap one in when you want the room kept as it is.** Hold `Ctrl` (`Cmd` on a Mac) and click, or choose **Open in place of the one in use** in the desk's menu: their terminal takes the place of the one you are in, the others keep their size, and no program redraws.
- **`Enter` on a desk** opens their terminal or goes to it, and puts nothing away.
- **In a narrow window,** where the floor and the terminals are never seen together, a click still shows one terminal at a time.

The first time you open a second terminal this way, a notice says what a second click does. If you had the habit of holding `Ctrl` to open one beside the others, a plain click does that now.

## On a Mac

- **`Cmd` is the key to hold with a click,** for a desk and for a file path in a terminal. A Control-click, which macOS treats as a right-click, now only opens the menu; before, on a desk it also opened or put away that desk's terminal.

## Fixed

- The input meter and the word-hint chips have their rounded ends.

Voice was checked in the real Windows app with the real speech model and a recorded clip fed at speaking pace: a panel opened over the terminal no longer ends the recording, and what was said meanwhile was typed. The cloud providers were checked against a pretend one on this computer, as before. The clicks and the microphone's menu were checked in a browser with demo data. Not checked: a live microphone by hand, and a real Mac, where the change was tested by having the browser say it was one.

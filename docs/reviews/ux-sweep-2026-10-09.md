# Agent Moshpit UX sweep, rerun on 9 October 2026

A second walk through Agent Moshpit, against the build with the 9 October fixes. It checks each fix listed in the first sweep ([ux-sweep-2026-10-08.md](ux-sweep-2026-10-08.md), "What was changed on 9 October") and looks for what is still missing. No app code was changed for this review.

**How it was tested**

- **Baselines.** `node tools/e2e/ui-demo.mjs` passed 80 of 80. `tools/e2e/terminals.mjs`, run against a copy of the debug build, passed all 38 checks.
- **Demo mode.** The built window (`dist/`, 9 October 01:18) was served with `vite preview` and driven in Edge through Playwright. Scenes: office, crowd, empty, plus `&problem`. Sizes: 1440×900, 1280×800, 820×560, 420×760 and 340×600.
- **The real desktop app.** A copy of the debug build from 9 October 01:18 ran as its own instance (`ux`), with its own data folder, driven over the webview's debugging port. The agents were these stand-ins, added through `harnesses.json`:
  - the repo's `fake-agent.mjs`;
  - a key probe that prints every byte it receives;
  - a program that asks "Do you trust the files in this folder?" before it starts;
  - a full-screen program that redraws everything on every resize;
  - a program that answers a task in two seconds.

  A stand-in `code.cmd` on the app's PATH recorded what Ctrl+click on a file path would have asked VS Code to do.
- **Real Claude Code 2.1.290 and Codex 0.161.0**: each was started once with no task, in a new folder. Nothing was typed into either.
- **Real input**, with the owner's go-ahead: real keystrokes, real right-clicks, and a real drag of a file from an Explorer window onto a pane, all sent to the test window only (see "Real input" at the end).

Severity and effort are as in the first sweep. Severity: **High** breaks a promise or loses your place, **Medium** is daily friction, **Low** is polish. Effort: **XS** under an hour, **S** half a day, **M** one to three days, **L** a week or more.

Screenshots are in [ux-sweep-2/](ux-sweep-2/). Numbers 01–33 are demo mode and 40–61 are the real app.

## 3. Findings by flow

### 1. First launch and the New agent form

**What happened.**
- The empty office now also teaches Ctrl+click ("Hold Ctrl as you click to open another beside it, as many as you like") ([32](ux-sweep-2/32-empty.png)).
- Programs that aren't installed sit behind "+5 to install".
- Start and Cancel stay at the foot of the form, and Ctrl+Enter starts ([29](ux-sweep-2/29-new-agent.png)).
- The program and the copy checkbox are remembered between agents.
- Names come from the task, without trailing joining words, and duplicates are numbered.
- The form lists "Also known, but installed their own way: Cursor CLI, Aider, Goose, Droid."
- A hand-added row shows its version as node's own ("Key Probe 24.15.0"), which is harmless but odd.

**What a vibe coder expects.** This is now right.

**Proposal.** Nothing for this flow. The demo bridge names agents its own way (first six words, no numbering); bring it in line so the demo shows what the app does (XS).

**Severity.** Low.

### 2. Status: someone needing you

**What happened.**
- **The hand stays up.** All six ways of looking away were tested with a stand-in that rings the bell (verdict 1).
- **Redraws aren't work** for programs that redraw on resize, and for Claude Code, whose status comes from its own session file.
- **Codex** still flashed "working" for about a second after its pane opened and after a zoom (`idle > working > idle`). With the window in front it settles as idle. If the window were behind at that moment, the same blip would end as "done".
- **Asked before starting** works when a task was given ([44](ux-sweep-2/44-real-asked-before-starting.png)), but not without one ([56](ux-sweep-2/56-real-claude-codex.png)).
- **Answering a hand while the window isn't focused** ends as "done" for a moment, though nothing was done. In daily use you type with the window focused, so this mostly shows up in tests.
- **"starting" counts as working** in the bar: "1 working" while a program was still starting ([51](ux-sweep-2/51-real-shift-tab-focus.png)).

**What a vibe coder expects.** Hands that only go up for real questions and stay up until answered. The trust question is a question.

**Proposal.**
- Detect untrusted folders for Claude Code and Codex before starting (top ten #6, S).
- End the redraw window when output pauses for about 300 ms, rather than at a fixed 1.2 s (S).
- Leave "starting" out of the working count (XS).

**Severity.** Medium.

### 3. Opening terminals, and the grid

**What happened.**
- Click, Ctrl+click and Ctrl+Enter on a desk work.
- Sizes are kept, and closing a pane gives its room to a neighbour (verdict 11).
- Dragging a header swaps two panes, with a clear highlight ([19](ux-sweep-2/19-dragging-header.png)).
- Away desks open without starting.
- The ⋯ menu has a new "Make the terminals even: 3 open, each an equal share".

**What a vibe coder expects.** This now matches Windows Terminal and Warp, plus the floor.

**Proposal.** None. Keep it.

**Severity.** —

### 4. Picking someone from the floor (Ctrl+\`)

**What happened.**
- After Ctrl+\`, a click on any desk brings the grid back with that desk in the pane that last had the keyboard, and Ctrl+click adds it beside the others ([14](ux-sweep-2/14-grid-back-with-new-desk.png)).
- The desk that was in that pane leaves the grid without a word. In the test, "Bump dependencies" was replaced by "Fix the checkout total".

**What a vibe coder expects.** Mostly this. Someone who opened four panes on purpose may not notice that one went.

**Proposal.** When a plain click from the floor replaces a pane, say so in a short toast, "Bump dependencies put away. Undo", or open the desk beside the others when there is room (XS).

**Severity.** Low.

### 5. Zoom

**What happened.**
- Ctrl+Shift+Enter, the button and "+3" work, and Alt+N while zoomed switches the zoomed pane.
- A double-click on the header mostly renames instead of zooming ([16b](ux-sweep-2/16b-header-dblclick-rename.png)).

**What a vibe coder expects.** A double-click on a title bar maximises. That is the one gesture everyone knows.

**Proposal.** Top ten #4.

**Severity.** Medium. Effort XS.

### 6. Keys inside a terminal

**What happened.**
- The probe received what it should for every key listed in verdict 8.
- The office now takes these keys, as designed:
  - Ctrl+= / - / 0;
  - Alt+1…9 (readline's digit argument in a shell, rarely used by agent CLIs);
  - Ctrl+Shift+W, N, Q, F, Enter, [ and ].
- **Shift+Tab** leaves the terminal (top ten #1). A second Shift+Tab walks back through the header's buttons, ending on zoom. From there, Enter or Space presses the button, and letters trigger the floor's shortcuts.

**What a vibe coder expects.** Every key the program uses stays in the program.

**Proposal.** In `TerminalView.svelte`'s key handler, catch Shift+Tab: send `\x1b[Z`, call `preventDefault`, and return false. Check the same for any other key that xterm, in screen-reader mode, sends without cancelling (XS).

**Severity.** High.

### 7. Putting a pane away, and the hint

**What happened.**
- × puts a pane away. The first time, a hint says: "Refactor auth middleware keeps working at their desk. To end their program, right-click the desk and choose Remove." It stays 9 s, at the bottom centre, over the panes' last lines ([33](ux-sweep-2/33-hint-covers-terminal.png)).
- Ctrl+Shift+W does the same from the keyboard, and focus moves to the next pane.

**What a vibe coder expects.** A hint that points at Stop, which is the item that ends a program and keeps the desk, and that doesn't cover the prompt.

**Proposal.**
- Change the hint to: "…keeps working at their desk. To end their program: right-click the desk → Stop."
- Move toasts off the panes (top ten #5).

**Severity.** Medium. Effort XS.

### 8. Copy, paste, pictures, links

**What happened.**
- **Ctrl+V with a picture** saves it under `pasted/` and pastes its path ([48](ux-sweep-2/48-real-picture-paste.png)). Claude Code and Codex both take a picture by its path. Text paste is unchanged.
- **Right-click → Paste** opens Edge's permission prompt (top ten #2).
- **Right-click → Copy** appears only when text is selected, which is right.
- **Find** (Ctrl+Shift+F) selects the match and says "Enter: older · Shift+Enter: newer" ([18](ux-sweep-2/18-find.png)).
- **Links:**
  - file paths are underlined on hover and open in VS Code with Ctrl+click ([49](ux-sweep-2/49-real-path-link.png));
  - a plain click teaches Ctrl+click, once;
  - refused web links say why.

**What a vibe coder expects.** Right-click → Paste as in Windows Terminal, with no prompt. Ctrl+click opens their own editor, here Cursor.

**Proposal.** Top ten #2 and #3. Not checked: whether a pasted picture's path is taken by real Claude Code and Codex, because that means typing into them.

**Severity.** Medium–High.

### 9. Drop a file on a pane

**What happened.**
- A file dragged from Explorer onto a pane lights that pane up, with a bar: "Drop to paste the paths into Drop target" ([60](ux-sweep-2/60-real-drag-over-pane.png)).
- On release, its path is pasted in quotes, because it has a space, and the keyboard goes to that pane ([61](ux-sweep-2/61-real-dropped-path.png)).
- This is what Windows Terminal does, and it is the usual way to hand Claude Code a screenshot when Alt+V fails.

**Proposal.** None. Keep it.

**Severity.** —

### 10. Desk menu, removing, renaming, restarting

**What happened.**
- **The menu.** One menu on the floor, in the strip and on the pane header, reached by right-click, Shift+F10 or ⋯ ([02](ux-sweep-2/02-desk-menu.png)). Arrow keys move through it, Esc closes it, and focus goes back to the desk. Its wording is honest per program: "It starts afresh" or "It carries on the conversation", "The desk stays; they start afresh next time".
- **Removing.** × on hover, middle-click and Delete all remove, with an 8 s Undo. During those 8 s the program is still running and Undo brings the desk back as it was. Busy desks ask first ([04](ux-sweep-2/04-remove-busy-asks.png)).
- **Restart and Stop** work from the floor without opening the pane. **Rename** works from F2, the menu, and a double-click on a pane title.
- **Small gaps:**
  - After Delete, focus goes to the page body, and the arrow keys do nothing until you click.
  - Remove's hint is cut ("…takes them off th…").
  - The menu says "a double-click on the name", but that only works on a pane's title, not on a desk.
  - Away desks still say "Not running. Open the desk to carry on." That no longer describes what opening does.
  - The terminal's right-click menu includes Restart and Stop with no confirmation, a few rows under Paste ([17](ux-sweep-2/17-term-menu.png)).
  - The menu has no "Open in Cursor/VS Code" for the folder; the first sweep proposed one.

**What a vibe coder expects.** This is what Windows Terminal and VS Code offer, and the Undo is better than a confirm box.

**Proposal.**
- Move focus to the next desk after a removal (XS).
- Change the away text to "Not running. Open to see where they left off" (XS).
- Ask before Restart or Stop when the agent is working (XS).
- Add "Open the folder in <editor>" once the editor is known (top ten #3).

**Severity.** Low–Medium.

## 5. What works well and must be kept

- **The raised hand is trustworthy now.** It survives every way of looking away and comes down only when you answer. This was the most important fix ([41](ux-sweep-2/41-real-hand-stays-on-floor.png), [42](ux-sweep-2/42-real-hand-after-put-away.png)).
- **The grid behaves like a grid.** Sizes are kept, closing gives room to a neighbour, a header drags to swap, and Ctrl+\` round trips keep the set ([11](ux-sweep-2/11-three-kept-sizes.png), [13](ux-sweep-2/13-closed-one-of-four.png), [19](ux-sweep-2/19-dragging-header.png)).
- **Remove with Undo.** No confirm box for idle desks, an 8 s Undo, the program kept alive until then, and a question only when the agent is busy. That is better than any of the four apps compared.
- **Away means away.** Opening a desk never starts a program; Carry on and Start again are always an explicit click. Relaunching shows where everyone left off.
- **Picture paste and file drop.** Win+Shift+S, then Ctrl+V into any agent, works for every program, not only those with their own image paste. A file dragged from Explorer lands in the pane under the pointer, which lights up first.
- **It no longer acts like a browser.** F5, Ctrl+R, Ctrl+P, Ctrl+S and F12 go to the program, and nothing reloads, prints or opens DevTools.
- **The strip is a real list**, and the dense floor fits 20 agents at 1440×900 ([20](ux-sweep-2/20-strip-list.png), [22](ux-sweep-2/22-crowd-20.png)).
- **Honest words** in the desk menu, the quit dialog and the close notice, about who carries on and who starts afresh.
- **The Keys sheet**, reachable by ?, Ctrl+Shift+/ and the ⋯ menu, lists exactly what works ([08](ux-sweep-2/08-keys.png)).
- **Everything from the first sweep's keep list** still holds ([01](ux-sweep-2/01-office.png)): real programs drawn faithfully, keys reaching the program (apart from Shift+Tab), the band, programs dying with the app, the floor's art and words.

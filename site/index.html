A small office for your coding agents.

Claude Code, Codex, Antigravity CLI and the rest run as the real programs, each in a terminal of its own inside one window. Each is drawn as a person at a desk, so one look tells you who is working, who is done, and who has a hand up because they need you.

Download

Free and open source. Available for Windows, macOS and Linux.

what you see/

6 states
  • needs you A hand up. It has stopped on a question only you can answer.
  • working Typing, with code on the screen.
  • done Leaning back. It finished something you have not looked at yet.
  • idle Sitting at a prompt, with nothing to do.
  • trouble Hands on their head. The program stopped with an error.
  • away Asleep. Not running; the desk keeps where they left off.

Their terminal is the real thing.

Click a desk and their terminal opens beside the floor. Everything under a pane's header is the program's own screen: its prompt, its slash commands, its approvals. The office draws none of it. You answer a question where the program asks it.

Open as many as you like, side by side or in a grid. Close the window and the agents keep working; the tray icon and a notification tell you when one needs you.

the facts/

Free
No price, no paid tier, no account to make.
MIT licensed
All of the source is on GitHub. Read it, build it, change it.
Your own CLIs, your own sign-ins
It starts the claude, codex or agy you already have, the way your terminal does. It keeps no API key and no model setting.
Sends nothing about you
The app has no telemetry and no analytics. All it asks the internet itself is whether a newer version is out, of your CLIs and of the app, and that can be turned off.

download/

3 systems

Windows

Windows 11, 64-bit. Windows 10 is untried.

  • Installer.exe
  • MSI package.msi

The first time: Windows says it protected your PC. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

Or in PowerShell, which asks nothing:

irm https://agentmoshpit.com/install.ps1 | iex

macOS

macOS 10.15 or later, Apple Silicon or Intel.

Paste this into Terminal:

curl -fsSL https://agentmoshpit.com/install.sh | sh

It puts Agent Moshpit in Applications and opens it. The script is short, if you want to read it first.

There is a .dmg too, for Apple Silicon and for Intel. macOS refuses to open an app from one until you allow it under System Settings, Privacy & Security. The command has no such step.

Linux

64-bit, with WebKitGTK 4.1.

  • Any distribution.AppImage
  • Debian, Ubuntu.deb
  • Fedora, openSUSE.rpm

The first time: an AppImage has to be made runnable: chmod +x the file, then start it.

Or in a terminal, which picks the right one:

curl -fsSL https://agentmoshpit.com/install.sh | sh

The app is not signed with a paid certificate yet, which is why Windows asks the first time and why macOS is given a command instead of a file to click. All downloads and the release notes are on GitHub. You also need at least one agent CLI, installed and signed in the way its makers say; the office can install the ones published on npm for you.
